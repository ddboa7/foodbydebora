Homemade, balanced and delicious vegan and vegetarian dishes that delight and have a lasting positive effect.

Welcome,

My name is Debora and, for me, food is far more than just sustenance. It is a conscious enjoyment that nourishes the body and the senses and makes you feel alive. 

Through my catering, I show that plant-based cuisine is not a sacrifice, but an enrichment that not only surprises and delights you with its flavours, but also leaves you feeling satisfied for the long term. 



Homemade, quality & locality

I place great value on craftsmanship, as it is reflected in the flavour: Everything is fresh and, for the most part, prepared from scratch. No ready-made mixes with additives. 

The ingredients are selected with great care. Even though we source as locally as possible, there are some treasures that I, as a passionate chef, simply love. I’m just saying chocolate, cardamom, and mango. This makes it all the more important to me to source these from producers who work fairly and sustainably. Some of the herbs even come from the own permaculture garden.



What makes my catering special

For me, it’s not just about taste, but also about how you feel after eating.

As a nutritionist with further training in Ayurveda and gut health, I place great importance on ensuring that my dishes are not only delicious, but also nutritious, balanced and easy to digest. 

With a high proportion of vegetables, proteins, ripe fruit, high-quality fats and carefully prepared legumes, I create meals that support your energy levels, digestion and long-term health.



Inspired by the world & lifelong learning

I’ve been passionate about cooking ever since my teenage years. I can lose myself in cookbooks for hours, wander through markets abroad and search for vegetables I’ve never seen before. I regularly experiment in the kitchen with spices, ingredients and kitchen tools to develop new dishes. 

Since I started eating vegan (2016), a whole new culinary world has opened up to me. It is full of diversity that continues to inspire me to this day. 

My cooking is characterised by flavours from Asia, the Middle East and the Mediterranean. I am inspired by the places I have been lucky enough to visit, and by the belief that good food brings people together and is essential for well-being.

My Affiliate programm cookbook

Whether you follow a vegan nutrition or are simply looking for new, creative alternatives, this book invites you to discover a variety of delicious dishes. I share my favourite recipes, including tiramisu, tom yum, refreshing and filling salads, countless aromatic sauces such as muhammara and ajvar, delicious drinks such as lassi and numerous basic recipes for bread, yoghurt and kimchi.

Most dishes can be prepared in less than 30 minutes and can be flexibly adapted to your preferences. The focus is on wholesome, balanced nutrition - without refined sugar. Many recipes are naturally gluten-free or can be prepared gluten-free, as well as soya-free and nut-free alternatives.

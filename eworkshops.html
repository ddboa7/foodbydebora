Cooking classes

To attend one of my cooking classes, you definitely do not have to follow a vegan or even vegetarian nutrition. Maybe you would like to integrate more vegetables, eat less meat, or simply learn how to prepare healthy AND delicious meals that do not take more than 30 minutes. Most of my recipes are very adaptable to your personal preferences or seasonal availability and invite you to get creative.

The workshops either take place at your home or in a rented room in Zug. If you would like to gift a workshop to someone, please contact me and I will prepare a voucher.

WHAT WILL YOU LEARN
You will learn how to create delicious and satisfying vegan dishes that you can easily integrate into your daily life, foundational principles for a balanced nutrition, how to preserve vitamins while cooking, cutting techniques, vegan replacements, and other tips and tricks. I am happy to incorporate your individual wishes.

DETAILS

  • Duration: 3-5 hours
  • Number of dishes: 3-7
  • Minimum number of participants: 2
  • Maximum number of participants: 12
  • No prior experience is required
  • Cost: CHF 120-180 per person (flexible pricing outside of Switzerland)
  • Possible types of cuisines: Asian, Mediterranean, Italian, Mexican, Oriental, European

Please let me know in advance if you have any allergies. All dishes are free of refined sugar.

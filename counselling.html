Personalised mentoring


I take a holistic approach to helping health-conscious people achieve lasting physical and mental wellbeing. My work isn’t about strict plans, calorie counting or perfection, but about helping you understand yourself better and develop a diet and lifestyle that supports your body, your nervous system and your daily life.

Everyone is different. That’s why I take a personalised approach, combining nutritional knowledge, psychology, support for the nervous system, practical routines tailored to you, reflection and strategies that work in everyday life.

Together, we look not only at what you eat, but also at:

  • why certain patterns keep recurring
  • what lies behind cravings, emotional eating and overeating
  • how stress, perfectionism and self-esteem affect your health
  • what your body is actually trying to tell you

Together, we’ll work to ensure that you ...

  • feel comfortable and balanced in your body again
  • develop habits that bring more inner peace and stability to your daily life
  • feel that you’re eating healthy
  • free yourself from the daily stress surrounding food and establish supportive routines
  • feel lighter physically and mentally, without constantly worrying about food
  • learn to look after yourself without feeling guilty
  • create a lifestyle that supports your energy, your nervous system and your wellbeing in the long term
  • develop healthier relationships
  • feel less easily overstimulated, exhausted or emotionally overwhelmed
  • gain greater clarity about your own path and shape a life that feels harmonious and fulfilling

Why I do this work

My journey towards holistic health began not only through my studies and further educations, but also through my own experiences.

I know from personal experience just how closely physical symptoms, stress, self-esteem, nutrition and mental wellbeing are linked. For many years, I have grappled with issues such as food cravings, restrictive eating, hormonal imbalances, bloating, autoimmune disorders, burnout and the search for inner balance.

Our bodies are constantly communicating with us. Symptoms are often not enemies, but valuable clues that something has become unbalanced. They urge us to rethink our lifestyle and habits before serious health problems arise.

Today, I combine psychological knowledge, holistic health, nutrition and practical everyday tools to support people on their own journey in a sustainable way.

I look forward to support you on your journey.
Debora
